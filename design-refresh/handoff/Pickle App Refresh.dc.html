Context

Question & answer
Which platforms would be affected if we split the Retail & Billing capability (CAP-006)?

Three platforms support CAP-006 today, via seven interfaces in INT-IFC:

PLAT-BIL — Billing engine · system of record for invoices · 3 interfaces PLAT-CRM — Customer platform · reads consumption, writes account status · 2 interfaces PLAT-MDM — Meter data management · half-hourly reads feed billing · 2 interfaces

A split would also touch two committed decisions (ADR-007, ADR-009) and the CRUD mapping in DAT-PRO-DAC. The cleanest seam is between metering operations and billing & payments — consistent with the direction already committed in ADR-007.

Smart Metering Rollout — Solution Intent

Approved
Author: Joe B Audience: Architecture & Engineering Board SVI-002 · v1.3

1. Problem statement

Half-hourly settlement requires meter reads at a volume the current batch pipeline cannot sustain. Billing accuracy targets and the regulator's settlement timetable are both at risk from FY27.

2. Solution overview

Introduce an event-streamed meter-data pathway from PLAT-MDM into billing and analytics. Fixed intent: the stream is the system of record for reads. Variable intent: whether settlement calculations move onto the stream in phase one or two.

3. Platforms involved

PLAT-MDM · meter data PLAT-BIL · billing engine PLAT-ANL · analytics

4. Risks & assumptions

Assumes the API gateway decision (ADR-008) lands before phase one. Event replay retention beyond 13 months is an open cost question. A guardrail review (INT-GRD) is required for the new streaming pattern.
