Why Pickle

A working architecture repository, not slideware

Every artefact is live, structured, and rendered directly from the Git repository. Nothing goes stale in a deck.

Architecture as Code

Enterprise architecture stored as structured, version-controlled data — not documents or spreadsheets. Every artefact is machine-readable JSON, validated against a schema. Changes require a formal Decision Record: no undocumented drift. Query, diff, and compare architecture state like source code.

Pickle gives you a structured, schema-validated architecture repository from day one.

Agentic AI Architecture

AI agents actively participate in the process, analysing and validating changes alongside your architects. Every decision is analysed across seven dimensions before it reaches a human. Analysis is structured and traceable — not free-form commentary. The human stays in the loop: AI informs, architects decide.

Every decision gets automated analysis before it reaches your architects.

Architectural Governance

A governed, traceable process for changing your architecture, driven by Decision Records and enforced through code review. Every change is justified in an Architecture Decision Record; Git history provides an immutable audit trail. Strategy, principles and guardrails are codified — not just documented.

The rigour of decision records with the practicality of pull requests.

The architecture model

Five domains · Three abstraction layers · Four output formats

Every piece of architecture content sits at the intersection of three axes. Domain answers "which part of the organisation", abstraction layer answers "how much detail", and format answers "what kind of content".

BUS

Business

The organisation's capabilities, processes, and operating model: what the business does and why.

DAT

Data

The data assets, structures, and governance that support business operations.

APP

Application

The software applications and platforms that deliver business capabilities.

INT

Integration

How systems and services connect and communicate: APIs, events, messaging.

SOL

Solution

Joined-up designs that span multiple domains to address a specific need.

What & Why

Conceptual

Sets direction and intent: what the architecture needs to achieve and why, without any technology choices.

How

Logical

The rules and principles that guide design decisions, without committing to any specific tool or product.

Where & With What

Physical

The concrete standards and technology decisions that govern how the architecture is built and operated.

Catalogues

Structured lists of architecture entities, validated by JSON Schema.

Diagrams

Visual representations of entities and relationships, rendered live from the repository.

Documents

Structured narrative content with multiple named instances: visions, intents, designs, specs.

Matrices

Grids mapping relationships between entity sets, spanning layers and domains.

The foundation, defined by your architects

Strategy, Principles & Guardrails

Three artefacts are the spine of the whole model: one per abstraction layer, in every domain. Your architects author them; the agents only check that changes respect them. This is where human judgement sets the rules.
